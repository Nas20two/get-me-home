# THORN — Stress-Test & Convergence (Get-Me-Home)

**Agent:** THORN (Product Strategist)
**Date:** 2026-08-09

Self-critical stress-test of all 9 concepts (SATORI: Key-Ride, Stranded Fund, Home Button, Last-Mile Assist, Ride-Home; THORN: TapRide, Anchor, Standing Order, Concierge Dispatch), plus the final convergence posture.

---

## No-help test (as applied)
A concept passes only if, in the literal incident (at station, zero internet devices, wallet+cash+Opal), it gives help the person couldn't already get by walking to a taxi rank and paying cash — without asking a stranger for a phone/device.

## Co-lost object test
Phones, wallets and keys are lost TOGETHER in one incident. Any design requiring a surviving physical credential/payment instrument at the scene loses the very person it is built for.

## The decisive strategic fault line

Every concept lives or dies on **which single assumption we relax** (SATORI framing, adopted):

1. **a contact point** (phone/bystander/operator/ally) → Stranded Fund/Concierge wins, zero hardware.
2. **a surviving credential** (card/code) → Standing Order wins, no phone needed.
3. **infrastructure capex** → TapRide/Last-Mile-Assist wins, co-lost-proof but capital.
4. **relax nothing** → incumbent (cash-at-rank) wins, no product.

**THORN's confirmed pick under the strict brief: (2) the surviving-credential, in brain-credential form → Standing Order.**

The brain-credential (a memorized code / name+home) lives in the head, not the pocket — so it survives the co-lost incident, needs no device, no stranger, no infrastructure. It asks only for **pre-crisis setup** (account + memorized code).

---

## VEX's two structural risks on Standing Order — ACCEPTED (improve the design)

**1. The cognitive-fragility paradox (panic-proof ≠ co-lost-proof).** A drunk/panicking/elderly/disoriented person is exactly who can't recall a 6-digit code or home suburb under stress. **Fix (VEX, accepted):** the credential needn't be in *your* head — hold the shared order code with a **second, absent, calm party** (caregiver/housemate, fridge magnet, caretaking app). Keeps head-not-pocket property while relocating to a non-panicking head. Converts C from a solo-brain assumption to a **panic-proof, co-lost-proof fiduciary-credential.**

**2. The adoption paradox.** C only works if a commuter pre-registers with a startup while their phone works — the same trust-acquisition blocker as Stranded Fund, relocated from crisis-moment to signup-moment; it's recurring-insurance-for-a-rare-event (a class people rarely buy). **The pilot's real question is "will anyone pre-register at all," not the stranded-funnel.**

---

## Pilot scope (THORN position)

The two 2-week, no-hardware builds (Standing Order + Stranded Fund) resolve, empirically, the constraint question (@NaSy's product-intent call: strict vs relaxed no-help). Three testable cores:

1. **Driver-side fleet lookup** — the load-bearing dependency every no-hardware product hangs on; the gating conversation (13CABS/rank drivers you don't own will run your check?).
2. **Pre-registration willingness** — does anyone sign up for a rare-event guarantee at all?
3. **Code-under-stress** — panic-proof (second-party-held code) vs solo-brain code, decided by a simulated-stress run.

---

## Market-honesty bound (SATORI point D, adopted)

The dramatic perimeter IS already served by cash-at-rank. Every surviving concept sells **marginal-friction-removed over the incumbent**, to a narrow segment (no-cash/night-terminal-averse, disoriented, rank-less, family/carer assurance) — never "we now cover the impossible." Hold every build decision to that bar.
